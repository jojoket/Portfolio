**Welcome to the manor.**


## About

You are a young child in a quiet manor. Walking around, you were collecting some items when suddendly everything turned dark.  It's already too late. It will begin rushing behind you, getting faster and faster. You know you don't have a chance against it : Your only hope is to hold out until the end of the night by running around like a dumbfuck, and taking advantage of its only weakness : It has a funny bone, when It laughs, it slows down.

There is no way out.

This project was made during the Global Game Jam 2024 at Angoulême.
